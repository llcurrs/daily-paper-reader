## 摘要
纵向临床队列具有多模态、采样不规则且普遍不完整的特点：在 ADNI 中，正电子发射断层扫描和脑脊液检测在大约一半的访视中缺失。线性状态空间模型能优雅地处理不规则采样，但通过掩蔽输入来处理缺失模态，而不改变转移算子。我们证明这是一种表示能力限制：任何转移算子不依赖于可用性模式的线性状态空间层，其隐状态都是可用性指示变量的加性函数，因此这类层无法表示两个模态共同存在或共同缺失之间的交互作用。我们提出 CAMOS，它为每个模态赋予一组二阶振荡器，这些振荡器通过一个位于微分方程内部且由可用性门控的矩阵进行耦合，从而使转移算子本身成为所采集测量项的函数。耦合会使非耦合振荡模型的分析失效，我们恢复了这种分析：逐通道的 Gershgorin 预算使有效刚度在所有 $2^M$ 种可用性模式和所有间隙上一致正定，一个能量论证将放大效应归因于可用性转变而非序列长度，并且通道分解保持了精确的结合性并行扫描。在 ADNI 上，CAMOS 在同次访视分期、landmark 预测和纵向预测方面优于非耦合振荡状态空间模型和临床融合模型，并且在零样本迁移到 OASIS-3 时，它是唯一避免坍缩到多数类的模型。

## Abstract
Longitudinal clinical cohorts are multimodal, irregularly sampled and pervasively incomplete: in ADNI, positron emission tomography and cerebrospinal fluid assays are absent from roughly half of all visits. Linear state-space models handle irregular sampling gracefully but treat a missing modality by masking the input, leaving the transition operator untouched. We prove that this is a representational limitation: the latent state of any linear state-space layer whose transition operator does not depend on the availability pattern is an additive function of the availability indicators, so no such layer can represent an interaction between two modalities being jointly present or jointly absent. We propose CAMOS, which gives each modality a bank of second-order oscillators coupled through a matrix that sits inside the differential equation and is gated by availability, so the transition operator itself becomes a function of which measurements were taken. Coupling invalidates the analysis of uncoupled oscillatory models, and we restore it: a per-channel Gershgorin budget makes the effective stiffness positive definite uniformly over all $2^M$ availability patterns and all gaps, an energy argument charges amplification to availability transitions rather than sequence length, and a channel factorization preserves exact associative parallel scans. On ADNI, CAMOS outperforms uncoupled oscillatory state-space models and clinical fusion models on same-visit staging, landmark prediction and longitudinal forecasting, and under zero-shot transfer to OASIS-3 it is the only model that avoids collapse to the majority class.
